BR-52
Dispute Eligibility &amp; Window
Disputes may be raised by EO or Worker within 14 days of shift completion. Minimum description: 50 characters. Evidence upload required for Worker-initiated payment disputes. Disputes after 14 days are rejected.
BR-53
Dispute Investigation SLA
CC must acknowledge a new dispute within 1 business day. Decision must be rendered within 7 business days. Unresolved disputes auto-escalate to Admin and are flagged in Customer Care Console.

BR-55
Dispute RS Penalty
Where a dispute ruling finds a worker at fault, −10 RS points is automatically applied upon CC or Admin issuing the decision. CC/Admin may waive the penalty with written justification.
BR-56
Dispute Evidence Retention
All dispute evidence is retained for 36 months from dispute closure. Parties may not delete submitted evidence. CC/Admin may seal evidence in cases involving legal proceedings.
BR-57
Dispute Queue Priority
Disputes ordered by: (1) Escalation-to-Admin flag (highest priority); (2) SLA risk (&lt;1 day remaining); (3) Submission date (oldest first). CC agents may self-assign disputes.
BR-58
Tickets classified: High (payment, safety, account lock) — first response within 4 hours; Medium (verification, dispute-adjacent) — within 1 business day; Low (general queries) — within 3 business days. SLA breach triggers auto-escalation to CC Team Lead.
BR-59
CC has jurisdiction over Level 1 dispute decisions, verification approvals, EO account approval, no-show overrides, and ticket handling. Final binding decisions, RS lock/unlock, and user banning escalate to Admin.
RS deductions: No-show = −25; Late cancellation (&lt;6h) = −15; Cancellation (6–24h) = −5; Dispute ruling against worker = −10. Recovery: +2 RS per completed shift with rating ≥ 4.0 stars. RS floor is 0, ceiling is 100.
